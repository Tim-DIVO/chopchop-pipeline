Ranking weights

Each factor is min–max normalised across the guides currently shown, multiplied by its weight, and summed. Set a weight to 0 to ignore it. Sort by Score to use this ranking.

Thresholds used by Kroll

Efficiency > 0.60 · frameshift > 80% (66% = random, below that is worse than chance) · MM0–MM3 all zero, but a single MM0 hit on an alt contig is an assembly ghost copy and can be ignored · no SNPs across the 23-mer in your strain.
